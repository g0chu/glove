# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as
contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and
our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Respect others and their work: be kind and assume good faith.
- Constructive criticism, focused on the code.
- No harassment, insulting or derogatory language, personal attacks, or
  uninvited personal attention.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by opening an issue or contacting the project maintainer
([@g0chu](https://github.com/g0chu)). All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the expected behavior and taking
appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any inappropriate
behavior. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, edits, and other contributions that are not
aligned to this Code of Conduct.
